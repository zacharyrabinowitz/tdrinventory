Import Items

Upload your inventory sheet (.xlsx, .xls, .csv, or .pdf). Columns are matched by header text: the unlabeled column right after the item name is On Hand, "Count" is the unit it's counted in, "Pars" is the target on-hand level, "order" queues an order. New items are created automatically (with a new unit or category if needed); items that already exist are never renamed/recategorized — only their Par gets refreshed and an order gets queued if the sheet has one.
